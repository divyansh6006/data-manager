Product Requirements Document
Internal Lead Management & Counseling CRM
For an Online MBA Education-Consulting Company
Version 1.0 — Phase 1 Scope
Status: In Design — Core Decisions Finalized, Screens Specified

1. Executive Summary
This document defines the requirements for an internal Lead Management CRM built for an education-consulting company that sells online MBA programs through a team of counselors. The company currently receives raw lead data from multiple sources (Naukri, LinkedIn, referrals, website forms) and manages distribution and follow-up manually through Excel, resulting in duplicate calling, lead leakage, unfair distribution, and no reliable reporting.
The product replaces this with a structured pipeline: leads are uploaded, cleaned, filtered, and distributed by a defined allocation method; counselors work leads through three qualification stages (L1, L2, L3); and every action is logged for accountability. The system is intentionally low-AI — the differentiator is workflow discipline, access control, and auditability, not machine learning.
Primary goal: prevent lead leakage and duplicate work, give managers real visibility into the pipeline, and keep the system maintainable long-term by a small team.

2. Objectives & Success Criteria
2.1 Objectives
Eliminate duplicate calling by enforcing single-owner lead assignment.
Give managers a fast, auditable way to distribute leads without spreadsheets.
Give leadership real-time visibility into pipeline health and counselor performance.
Prevent data leakage (copy/paste, exports, screenshots) as a first-class design constraint, not an afterthought.
Keep the system maintainable by a small internal team or a lean external retainer.
2.2 Success Criteria (Phase 1 launch)
100% of new leads enter the system via upload — no more leads distributed by raw Excel/WhatsApp/manual handoff.
Every lead has exactly one owning counselor at any point in time, enforced by the system (not by policy alone).
Every tick, cross, remark, transfer, and export is logged and attributable to a user and timestamp.
Managers can distribute a filtered batch of leads in under 2 minutes using Manual Count Allocation.
All logins are restricted to company email domain accounts; no personal email access is possible.

6. Stage Definitions — L1 / L2 / L3
L1 — Qualification
Fields: Interested (Y/N), Working professional (Y/N), Rough budget, Eligible (Y/N)
Outcomes: Interested → L2 | Not Interested / Wrong Number / Duplicate / Invalid → Closed (mandatory reason dropdown)
L2 — Counseling & Follow-up
Fields: University discussed, course, fee/EMI discussed, follow-up date, remark
Can loop (Follow-up 1, 2, 3...) with a defined cap before auto-moving to Cold/Lost
Follow-up due dates auto-surface the lead to the top of the counselor's dashboard
L3 — Closure
Fields: University finalized, documents received (checklist), application submitted, admission confirmed, revenue/fee collected
Final status: Converted / Lost / On Hold
Connects revenue reporting back to lead source, enabling source-level ROI analysis

7. Distribution Logic — Manual Count Allocation (Decided, Phase 1)
Flow:
Manager filters the batch → sees total matching count.
Screen shows each counselor with current unworked load + an editable count input.
System validates allocated total equals available total, or leftover is explicitly marked unassigned.
On confirm, leads are pulled in a defined sort order and assigned atomically — full batch succeeds or none does.
Every distribution is logged: who, when, how many, to whom.
Edge cases handled:
Leads lock the moment they enter a draft distribution — prevents two managers double-assigning the same pool.
Assigning to an inactive/on-leave counselor is blocked by default, with override + confirmation.
Manager undo allowed only while a lead is untouched; once worked, correction requires the Team Leader transfer flow.
Leads sorted meaningfully (experience or upload order) before manual split.
Deferred to later phases: round-robin (continuous flow), tiered seniority-based split, and performance-weighted allocation — revisited once real conversion data exists.

8. Reassignment / Transfer Approval Flow (Decided)
Principle: once a counselor has made first contact, ownership can only change via request → approval → transfer. No direct counselor-to-counselor handoffs — a Team Leader is always in the loop.
Trigger types:
Counselor requests to give up a lead (reason via dropdown: language mismatch, wrong specialization, overloaded, candidate requested someone else).
Counselor requests to receive a specific lead (e.g. personal referral) — requires manager sign-off.
Team Leader/Manager-initiated (leave/exit, load rebalancing, performance intervention).
Request (reason + note + optional target) → Team Leader queue (Pending)   → Lead stays workable by current owner during this window   → Team Leader approves / rejects / reassigns to someone else   → On approval: full history travels with the lead, old owner loses access   → Logged: requested_by, reason, approved_by, old_owner, new_owner, timestamp
No auto-approval, ever — always a human sign-off.
Approved transfers aren't reversible by the requester — a reversal is a new request.
Pending requests unreviewed after 24 hours auto-escalate to the Manager.

9. Security & Anti-Leakage Requirements
Login restricted to @company.com via Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 SSO — no personal email access possible.
One active device/session per counselor; new login kills the old session.
Right-click, text-select, copy, cut, paste, print, and screenshot-key disabled on lead views. Note: 100% screenshot prevention is not achievable in a browser — watermarking (counselor name + timestamp) is the realistic deterrent.
Full phone number masked until officially assigned; calling routed through click-to-call (Exotel/Knowlarity) so the raw number is never dialed manually or copyable.
Export/download restricted to Super Admin; every export logged with who/when/how many rows.
Session timeout after a configurable idle period.
Full audit trail: every view, tick, cross, remark, and reassignment timestamped and attributed.


10. Screens & UI Requirements
All screens share a consistent visual system: dense, professional ops-software tone (not consumer-app styling), a neutral gray/white palette with one consistent accent color, a persistent left sidebar for navigation, and color-coded status badges (grey = raw, blue = assigned, green = converted, red = lost/invalid, amber = follow-up due).
10.1 Login Flow
Single shared entry point for all roles — no role picker. Role is determined server-side from the authenticated account and the user is redirected automatically to their role's dashboard.
Centered login card: company logo, primary "Sign in with Google Workspace" button, secondary Microsoft 365 option.
Domain restriction note visible: "Only @company.com accounts can access this system."
Error state for wrong-domain login attempts.
Device/session-conflict modal when signing in on a new device while another session is active.
Brief branded loading transition before redirecting into the role-specific dashboard.
10.2 Super Admin Dashboard
System-health and governance view. Nav: Dashboard, Leads Workspace (oversight), Lead Distribution (oversight), User & Role Management, Security & Audit Log, System Settings.
Server/system status, database row counts, SSO sync status.
Active security policy list with ENFORCED/PARTIAL/DISABLED badges.
Database backup log (ID, date, size, status).
System compliance checklist panel.
10.3 Manager/Ops Dashboard
Daily operations view. Nav: Dashboard, Lead Upload, Lead Filtering & Distribution, Lead Management, Counselor & Team Management, Reports & Analytics, University Master Data.
Today's upload summary with clean/duplicate/invalid breakdown.
Leads pending distribution, with a direct "Distribute Now" action.
Team-wide conversion funnel (L1→L2→L3→Converted).
Counselor load-balance snapshot flagging over/under-loaded counselors.
Operational activity feed (uploads, distributions, closures).
10.4 Team Leader Dashboard
Team-scoped management view. Nav: Dashboard, My Team's Leads, Transfer Approval Queue, Team Performance, Team Roster.
Transfer/reassignment approval queue front-and-center with SLA countdown badges.
Per-counselor pipeline breakdown for the team only.
Team conversion rate, week-over-week.
Follow-ups-due-today list aggregated across the team.
10.5 Counselor Dashboard
The highest-frequency screen in the system — optimized for speed. Nav: My Leads, Follow-ups Due, My Performance only (no admin/upload/distribution options visible).
Summary strip: counts by stage (L1/L2/L3) plus follow-ups-due-today.
Working table with inline tick/cross/remark actions per row — no page navigation required to process a lead.
Click-to-call icon instead of a visible phone number.
"Request Transfer" action opening a reason-dropdown modal.
Personal performance strip (own numbers only, no team comparison).
10.6 Lead Upload
Drag-and-drop/file-picker for Excel/CSV.
Column-mapping preview after upload with manual remap option.
Summary strip before import confirmation (total/clean/duplicate/invalid counts, each drillable).
Loading and success states, with a link into Lead Management for the new batch.
10.7 Lead Filtering & Pool View
Combinable filter bar: location, experience range, source, upload date range, course interest, status.
Results table with a running match count.
"Send to Distribution" primary action; saved filter presets.
Empty state for no matching leads.
10.8 Lead Distribution (Manual Count Allocation)
Available-pool count shown at top.
Per-counselor table: current load + editable allocation input.
Live-updating Available / Allocated / Remaining totals.
Confirm button disabled until totals reconcile; confirmation modal before atomic submit.
Validation/error state for mismatched totals; distinct treatment for on-leave counselors.
10.9 Counselor & Team Management
Counselor table: name, role, team, status, current load, quick actions.
Detail drawer per counselor: pipeline breakdown, recent activity, profile info.
Add Counselor action; search/filter by team, status, role.
10.10 Transfer/Reassignment Approval Queue
Queue of pending requests: lead, requester, reason, target, time pending, SLA countdown badge.
Approve/Reject actions requiring a note.
Tabs: Pending My Review / Escalated to Manager / Resolved History.
Empty state: "No pending transfer requests."
10.11 Reports & Analytics
Date-range picker.
Conversion funnel by team/counselor/source.
Counselor leaderboard (admissions, talk-time, follow-ups).
Lead-source ROI comparison.
Export controls with a visible "this export will be logged" indicator.
10.12 Lead Management / Master Lead Repository
System-wide summary strip: total leads, stage breakdown, unassigned count emphasized.
Filter/group by upload batch and date, stage, status, counselor, source, team.
Full table: name, masked phone, email, city, experience, source, upload date/batch, counselor, stage, status, last activity.
Row click opens full lead detail + complete activity timeline + stage-transition history.
10.13 University & Course Master Data
CRUD table: university, courses, fees, eligibility, specializations.
Add/Edit/Delete actions with detail modal for course/fee editing.
10.14 Audit Log / Security View
Searchable/filterable log: timestamp, user, action type, target record, note.
Filters for date range, user, action type; prominent search.
Technical, console-like visual tone distinct from the rest of the app.
10.15 Settings
Company email domain restriction config.
Session timeout duration.
Role & permission matrix editor.
Integrations: Exotel/Knowlarity API keys, SSO configuration.
Independent Save action per section.


11. Tech Stack
Frontend: React
Backend: Node.js (NestJS or Express)
Database: PostgreSQL
Cache/Queues: Redis (distribution logic, follow-up reminders)
Auth: Google Workspace SSO or Microsoft 365 SSO
Calling: Exotel or Knowlarity (click-to-call + recording)
Hosting: AWS or DigitalOcean, daily automated Postgres backups
File processing: Server-side Excel/CSV parsing and validation
This stack is deliberately conventional — an internal ops tool that a small team maintains for years benefits from boring, well-understood technology over novelty.

Decided
Distribution logic (Phase 1): Manual Count Allocation (Section 7).

15. Final Product Definition — Phase 1 Acceptance Criteria
This section defines what "done" means for Phase 1 launch. The product is considered complete and ready for pilot rollout when every item below is true.
Access & Roles
☐  Login works only via company-domain SSO; no personal email can authenticate.
☐  Each of the 4 roles is routed automatically to its own distinct dashboard on login — no manual role switcher exists anywhere in the UI.
☐  A Counselor cannot view, edit, or query any lead not currently assigned to them.
☐  A Manager/Team Leader can only see leads/counselors within their own scope (team-level, not company-wide, for Team Leaders).
Upload & Data Quality
☐  Excel/CSV upload correctly parses all required fields and flags duplicate/invalid rows before import is confirmed.
☐  Every upload creates a traceable batch record (who, when, row counts) visible in Lead Management.
Distribution
☐  Manual Count Allocation distributes a filtered batch atomically — partial/failed distributions never leave leads unassigned or duplicated.
☐  Two managers cannot simultaneously distribute overlapping leads from the same filtered pool.
Pipeline & Transfers
☐  Every lead can be tracked through L1 → L2 → L3 with all required fields captured at each stage.
☐  Transfer requests cannot bypass Team Leader approval; approved transfers carry full history to the new owner.
☐  Transfer requests unreviewed after 24 hours visibly escalate to the Manager queue.
Security & Audit
☐  Copy, cut, paste, right-click, and print are disabled on all lead-data views.
☐  Phone numbers are masked according to the agreed masking approach; calls route through click-to-call.
☐  Every tick, cross, remark, transfer, export, and login event is captured in the Audit Log with user and timestamp.
☐  Exports are restricted to Super Admin and are individually logged.
Reporting
☐  Manager and Team Leader dashboards reflect real-time (or near-real-time) pipeline counts.
☐  Reports can be filtered by date range, team, counselor, and lead source.
Operational Readiness
☐  Daily automated database backups are running and verified.
☐  A named owner (in-house or retainer) is assigned for post-launch maintenance before pilot rollout begins.
